Introduction

I was always a pretty big fan of history, especially in school. I think there was a part of it that was storytelling, and learning about the past that I really enjoyed. Especially, when I was younger, we got to learn about the "birth of the world", the history of our country, and other historical events. It did fascinate me, I've always liked knowing things about the world. Another aspect I liked was the preservation of history. Be it through paintings, music or otherwise, there was just something truly magical about the creative expression of people who lived in those times. Something really captivating about seeing these things that exist to essentially document the past.

Does history repeat itself, or do we repeat history?
