Dear diary,

I think its time I get this off my head. Out of my head, whatever the phrase is. I got a call a few months ago saying that one of my best friends, Jamal, had decided to move away and had cut all contact from everyone, including his family. This was already a bit unusual, Jamal was a very social person, he had lots of friends and connections. He owned a small business selling old furniture and other vintage items. Why would he just end up leaving? I tried to call him a few times but not once did he answer the phone. I called his sister, who he had a close bond with and she said that she hadn't heard from him in quite a while. I ended up asking her if she had his girlfriends phone number, and that I would try and reach out to her about him. She said she did and that she would give me her number. I called Jamals girlfriend, and she said that he had just suddenly broke up with her a few mornings ago, packed his stuff and disappeared. Jamal had been with her for nearly 7 years at that point, it was a very sudden and unexpected breakup. I asked her if she had any idea where he was, she said she couldn't say for sure, but she had a feeling that he still went to the old fishing shed down the river, where he would often go to fish and relax after work. I thanked her for telling me and hung up. I didn't go down there that day, but about a week later I decided I would go down and just see if he was there, or if there was any sign of him there. Now the river was quite long, and the shed was about a 15 minute drive away. I left at about 1600 hours, it was winter, so the sun hadn't started to set yet, but it was getting close. Admittedly, I should have left earlier, but it didn't occur to me at the time that when I got there, it would be dark soon. The drive wasn't really all that exciting, no cars were on the road, and the sun was not shining. I wasn't really paying much attention to the road, more just stuck in my own head. I couldn't remember making a wrong turn, but I somehow ended up arriving at the shed about 30 minutes later than I wanted to be. It was starting to get dark.
